## Operator checklist

Each item below is pending until its receipt identifies the actual artifact,
method, result and reviewer where applicable. Mark a skipped/unsupported optional
step explicitly. Record failed attempts without overwriting accepted work.

- [ ] **Preserve sources:** original song/video, edit project, references and
  completed workspace are located and hashed; an independent restored copy is
  checked. Keep creative packets, conversation exports and contributor/tool
  records private, with actual origins and capture dates.
- [ ] **Inspect:** video streams/frames/timing/color and original audio
  format/channels/sample count/duration/signal evidence are observed. Unsupported
  native analysis is explicit; compatibility derivatives have their own identity.
- [ ] **Choose optional video operations:** exact repair ranges and upscale
  configuration are selected. Adopt valid existing work; preserve clean frames
  and source mapping. Invisible-cleanup experiments stay under #39 and can be
  skipped without blocking a release.
- [ ] **Choose optional audio operations:** no processing, gain/finishing or an
  enhancement candidate is explicitly selected. Keep original and candidates;
  compare at matched volume and review stereo/phase, artifacts and the tail.
- [ ] **Select approved sources:** record exact audio/video identities, source
  clocks and A/V mapping. Review the full work, character/scene continuity and
  transitions before claiming an approved master.
- [ ] **Assemble and verify:** frame/sample cardinality, decode, dimensions,
  rational timing, color, channel order, offset and complete audio tail pass their
  declared checks. Silent AAC replacement or shortest-stream truncation cannot
  impersonate approved-source preservation.
- [ ] **Choose destination copies:** retain archival masters. Export only selected
  TikTok/SoundCloud/DistroKid versions, record format/precision/rate/gain/codec
  changes, recheck live destination/route requirements and validate actual bytes.
- [ ] **Creator attribution:** reviewed public artist/title/credit metadata is
  selected. Visible logos remain off. An optional invisible mark is accepted
  only after final-codec detection and visual/audio/stereo quality review;
  skip/no-go remains valid.
- [ ] **Optional C2PA:** preserve prior credentials/history, inspect actual trust
  status and sign only if a separately selected supported profile is available.
  Trusted C2PA remains deferred; self-signed credentials are not verified ownership.
- [ ] **Seal evidence:** after all byte edits/signing, record exact final SHA-256
  identities. Retain fixed subject manifest, source/master/delivery files, .ots
  proofs and separate pending/upgrade/verification receipts. No proof state is
  promoted solely from file existence or calendar submission.
- [ ] **Restore evidence:** recover one matching file/proof pair and verify it
  using a recorded method. Keep the proof and original bytes together. Record
  backup/verification failures as failures.
- [ ] **Prepare the posting package:** exact approved file, public credits/artwork
  references, destination profile, review and provenance locators are selected.
  Private source metadata, tokens, signing keys and unreleased creative material
  stay outside the public package.

Preparation does not publish. After a separately requested actual post, retain
the destination URL/time/route and a lawful post-upload spot-check when available.
Platform-generated bytes differ from our final file; compare quality and any
recoverable credentials/watermark separately. Do not infer platform survival
from a local transcode.

## Evidence and stopping conditions

Stop the affected branch on a missing source, ambiguous timeline, unsupported
profile, incomplete output, failed decode, lost stereo/tail, unacceptable quality
or unexplained lineage. Preserve good work and the failed attempt. A missing
optional watermark/signing provider instead yields skip/unavailable and permits
a clean candidate if ordinary acceptance passes.

Report measured facts, human review, detector observations and timestamp trust
as distinct results. This checklist cannot make a work copyrightable or prevent
copying; rights review considers the actual human expression and applicable law.

Before turning a manual step into automation, retain its exact successful recipe,
source/output identities and failure cases. Route execution through existing
Aniflow providers/stages and shared evidence; Flow owns cross-tool publication.
Qualification, packaging and product releases are later explicit gates.
